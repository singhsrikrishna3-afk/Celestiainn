THP — The Higher Plane

Shangarh, Sainj Valley, Kullu District, Himachal Pradesh

Experience the tranquility of Sainj Valley at our fully operational wooden homestay. Nestled in the magical meadowlands of Shangarh, THP (The Higher Plane) offers breathtaking panoramic views of pine-forested mountains and snowy Himalayan ranges.

Constructed in traditional Himachali timber architecture, this cozy lodge features warm, glowing lights, flower boxes on wooden balconies, a stone-paved garden pathway, and a warm hearth. It is the perfect mountain sanctuary for travelers seeking peaceful retreat, local trekking, and home-cooked organic Himachali meals.
