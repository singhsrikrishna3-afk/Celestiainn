# Celestia Inn — Corporate Brand Kit & Guidelines

Welcome to the official brand identity assets for **Celestia Inn**.

---

## Folder Structure

### `01-Natural-Editions/` (Primary Brand Identity)
- `01_Celestia_Inn_Pine_Cedar_Master.jpg` — The flagship natural master render on deckle cotton paper.
- `01_Celestia_Inn_Pine_Cedar_Vector.svg` — Infinite resolution vector logo in Himalayan Pine Green & Cedar Timber.
- `02_Celestia_Inn_Spruce_Badge_Master.jpg` — Circular outdoor heritage badge in deep spruce green & snow white.
- `03_Celestia_Inn_Charcoal_Stone_Master.jpg` — Artisanal mineral charcoal master on warm stone paper.
- `03_Celestia_Inn_Charcoal_Vector.svg` — Scalable vector for monochrome print, stamps, and linen embroidery.

### `02-Corporate-Vector-Logos/`
- `Celestia_Inn_Logo_Stacked.svg` — Primary vertical / stacked emblem and wordmark.
- `Celestia_Inn_Logo_Horizontal.svg` — Wide horizontal lockup for letterheads, email footers, and invoices.
- `Celestia_Inn_Emblem_Mark.svg` — Standalone circular medallion (ideal for wax seals and keycards).
- `Celestia_Inn_Favicon.svg` — High-contrast app icon and browser tab mark.

### `03-Mockups-And-Presentations/`
- High-resolution real-world mockups including architectural Himalayan slate signage, guest stationery, brass keycards, and presentation boards.

| :--- | :---: | :--- |
